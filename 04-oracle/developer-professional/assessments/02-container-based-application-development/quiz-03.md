## Questions

1. A junior engineer has "manage repos" IAM policy in a compartment and assumes that's also enough to edit that region's global image retention policy. Are they right? _(retention policy administration · exam-ready)_
2. Two repositories in different regions share the exact same display name. Is this allowed? Why or why not? _(repository naming scope · exam-ready)_
3. What specifically does an OCI Auth Token authenticate, and what does it *not* do on its own — answer using the vocabulary of a real error code. _(auth token scope · exam-ready)_
4. Why might a platform team deliberately choose *not* to enable `is-repository-created-on-first-push`, even though it removes a manual setup step for every new service? _(pipeline configuration trade-off · production-reasoning)_
5. In the walkthrough, the team pushes both `v2.4.0` and `stable-prod` pointing at the same digest as `9f3c2ab`. Does this count as three images against the 100,000-images-per-repository quota? _(tags vs. quota accounting · exam-ready)_
6. A deployment manifest references an image by tag (`:stable-prod`) rather than by digest. Concretely, what future event could change what that manifest deploys without anyone touching the manifest file? _(tag mutability risk · production-reasoning)_
7. Why does Exempt Versions support wildcard patterns like `prod-*` instead of requiring every tag to be listed individually? _(retention policy design · production-reasoning)_
8. Contrast a global image retention policy and a custom image retention policy on exactly two axes: how many can exist, and what each defaults to. _(retention policy taxonomy · exam-ready)_
9. An OKE pull secret (`ocirsecret`) is built from one specific engineer's personal Auth Token. What operational risk does this create that a resource-principal-based pipeline credential doesn't have? _(credential lifecycle risk · production-reasoning)_
10. Why does the lesson recommend deploying by digest rather than by the `stable-prod` tag in the walkthrough, even though the tag is more human-readable? _(digest pinning rationale · exam-ready)_
11. A repository accumulates 40,000 small images over a year with no retention policy, each roughly 5 MB. Roughly how much storage does that consume, and is that team closer to the 500 GB ceiling or the 100,000-image ceiling? _(quota arithmetic · production-reasoning)_
12. What's the actual mechanical difference between a Bearer Token and a Security Token (UPST), beyond both being "short-lived"? _(auth mechanism internals · exam-ready)_
13. Why can't a single custom image retention policy apply to two different regions at once? _(retention policy scope · exam-ready)_
14. Does marking a repository immutable affect how OKE pulls images from it? Why or why not? _(immutability scope · production-reasoning)_
15. Why is regional scoping (no cross-region default) described as a "recurring OCI pattern" rather than something unique to the registry? _(regional scoping · production-reasoning)_

2. Allowed — a repository is region-scoped, and the lesson never states whether the tenancy-wide name-uniqueness rule (§1.2) applies per-region or across all regions simultaneously. — **Partial**: §1.3 establishes regions as fully independent ("a repository created in `us-ashburn-1` simply does not exist in `us-phoenix-1`"), which implies per-region uniqueness, but §1.2's uniqueness statement ("unique across every compartment in the tenancy") doesn't explicitly resolve whether that scope is per-region or tenancy-wide-across-regions — a reader could reasonably take it either way.
3. An Auth Token authenticates *who you are*; it does not by itself authorize *what you can do* — a valid token with no `repos` policy grant is denied, commonly surfacing as a 404 rather than a 403. — **Covered**: §2.1 Nuance box.
4. Auto-created repositories always land in the tenancy's root compartment, which can put them outside the scope of a compartment-scoped IAM policy the platform team relies on — enabling the flag trades a manual step for a governance gap. — **Covered**: §5.1 Nuance box.

6. A future push that reuses the `stable-prod` tag on different content silently repoints what that manifest resolves to, with no manifest edit at all. — **Covered**: §3.2, directly restated for this scenario in §6.2.
7. It lets one field protect an entire release naming convention (e.g., every `prod-` prefixed tag, ever) without hand-listing each release as it's cut. — **Covered**: §4.3, illustrated by example.
8. Global: exactly one per region, created implicitly, defaults to retaining everything. Custom: any number of repositories can be covered but only one custom policy applies per repository at a time, and it must be explicitly created — no default retention behavior of its own beyond what you configure. — **Covered**: §4.1.
9. If that engineer's token is rotated or revoked (e.g., on offboarding), the cluster's pull secret silently breaks for every workload depending on it — a resource-principal credential has no single human owner to depart. — **Missing**: the lesson shows the `imagePullSecret` built from an Auth Token (§5.2) but never flags the personal-ownership/offboarding risk this creates, despite covering token rotation risk in a different context (§2.1).
10. A tag can be silently repointed at different content later; a digest is permanent, so pinning by digest guarantees the deployed content never changes without an explicit manifest edit. — **Covered**: §6.2.
11. 40,000 × 5 MB ≈ 200 GB — well under the 500 GB storage ceiling (§1.3), but at 40% of the 100,000-image count ceiling; this team is closer to the image-count limit than the storage limit. — **Partial**: the lesson provides both quota figures needed to compute this, but doesn't work this specific arithmetic itself — it only works the inverse case (large images hitting storage first) in §4.4.
12. A Bearer Token (JWT) is generated from an existing API-key-based OCI principal already authenticated conventionally; a Security Token (UPST) is obtained through a token-exchange step under Workload Identity Federation, letting an identity with no OCI API key at all get in. — **Partial**: §2.2 states this at the "who obtains it and how" level but doesn't go further into the exchange mechanics themselves.
13. Because a repository — and therefore anything governing it — is a regional resource; a policy created in one region has no reach into another region's separate set of repositories. — **Covered**: §4.1 ("the policy itself is region-scoped like everything else in this lesson").
14. No — immutability only blocks a *push* that would overwrite an existing tag; it says nothing about read access, so a pull proceeds exactly as it would against a mutable repository. — **Missing**: the lesson defines immutability purely in terms of blocking overwrite-on-push (§3.3) and never states explicitly that pull behavior is unaffected — a reader could plausibly (and wrongly) assume "immutable" restricts reads too.
15. Because Module `01` named the identical regional-scoping caveat for DevOps projects — the pattern (a resource type existing only in the region it was created in, with no automatic cross-region reach) recurs across OCI services rather than being registry-specific. — **Covered**: §1.3.

## Resolved

2026-07-20 — Three gaps closed after verification and lesson edits:
- **Q1** (retention-policy admin permission): verified against Oracle docs — tenancy-level `manage` is required to edit a global or custom policy's own criteria; repository-level `manage` only attaches/detaches a repo from an existing policy. Added to §4.1 and Practical Limits.
- **Q9** (personal-token offboarding risk): added as a Nuance in §5.2, in the Summary, and as a Practical Limits bullet — an `imagePullSecret` built from one engineer's Auth Token breaks cluster-wide on that token's rotation or the person's departure.
- **Q14** (immutability's push-only scope): added as a Nuance in §3.3 and folded into the Summary — immutable repositories block overwriting pushes only; pulls are unaffected.

Two gaps left **open, deliberately not guessed**:
- **Q2** (repo-name uniqueness scope): re-checked against `docs.oracle.com/.../registryconcepts.htm`, which states names are "unique across all compartments in the entire tenancy" but never explicitly resolves whether that scope spans regions or is per-region — genuinely ambiguous in the source docs, so nothing was added to the lesson rather than asserting an unconfirmed reading.
- **Q5** (tag-vs-quota accounting): re-checked across multiple doc pages; Oracle's documentation does not state whether a digest carrying several tags counts once or multiple times against the 100,000-images-per-repository quota. Left unaddressed in the lesson pending an authoritative source (or direct testing against a tenancy).

Q11 and Q12 (quota arithmetic and Bearer-vs-UPST internals) were ranked minor and left open — both are "apply the lesson's own facts one step further" gaps, not missing content.
